# Internal Mobility & Team Change Policy

- **Eligibility**: 12 months in current role; "Meets Expectations" or higher rating; not on a PIP or notice period.
- **Process**:
  1. Discuss intent with current project lead.
  2. Apply via the MyRSystems career portal.
  3. Complete the hiring manager's technical screening.
  4. Obtain approval from both current and receiving project managers.
