## Remote Work & WFH Policy
Associates are expected to operate from their designated ODC (Offshore Development Center) or approved base location. WFH (Work From Home) is permitted up to 2 days per week with prior approval from the Delivery Manager.

## Work from Abroad
Associates may work from an international location for up to 2 weeks per year, strictly contingent upon client data sovereignty agreements and visa compliance.

## Office Attendance
Tuesdays and Thursdays are mandatory in-office days across all India locations (Bangalore, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai) to foster collaboration and team synergy.

## Project Phoenix Overview
Project Phoenix is a multi-year digital transformation and legacy migration engagement for Meridian Corp, operating under our Global Delivery Model (GDM).

## Budget & Timeline
The Total Contract Value (TCV) is $2.4M, with Phase 1 deliverables targeted for a Q3 2026 go-live.
