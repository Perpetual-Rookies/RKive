# Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) Policy

- **Objective**: A structured process to assist associates in meeting performance expectations when consistent gaps are identified.
- **Identification**: Initiated after failure to meet KPIs or core competencies over a sustained period (e.g., two consecutive months).
- **Process**: The PIP document defines specific performance gaps, expectations, required resources, and a timeline of 30-60 days.
- **Review**: Mandatory weekly check-ins with the manager are required to monitor progress and provide feedback.
