# Leave and Attendance Policy

## Leave Entitlement

- **Annual Leave**: Associates are entitled to 24+ days of annual leave per year, with an additional day accrued for each year of completed service.
- **Medical Leave**: The company provides a private medical plan that includes 3 days of "no-questions-asked" leave per year.
- **Compensatory Off (Comp-Off)**: If an associate is required to work on a weekend or public holiday for critical project delivery, they are eligible for a Comp-Off, which must be availed within 30 days of the date worked.
- **Maternity/Paternity Leave**: R Systems provides 26 weeks of paid maternity leave and 2 weeks of paid paternity leave in accordance with statutory guidelines.

## Attendance Guidelines

- **Mandatory Office Days**: All India locations (Bangalore, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai) require in-office presence on **Tuesdays and Thursdays** to foster team synergy.
- **Remote Work**: Work From Home (WFH) is permitted up to 2 days per week, subject to prior approval from the Delivery Manager via the internal portal.
- **Shift Logging**: Associates working in shift rotations must log their start and end times in the MyRSystems portal daily to ensure accurate payroll processing.
