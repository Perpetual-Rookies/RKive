# Exit & Resignation Policy

- **Notice Period**: Employees must provide written notice as per their employment contract (typically 60 to 90 days) via the MyHR section of the employee portal.
- **Exit Interview**: All departing employees are requested to participate in a confidential exit interview with the HR Business Partner.
- **Clearance**: Employees must return all company IT assets, ID badges, and corporate cards, and obtain "No Dues" clearance from manager, IT, Finance, and HR before final settlement is processed.
- **F&F Settlement**: The final settlement, including leave encashment, is processed within 45 days of the last working day.
