# RSystems Ltd. - Service Offerings

We provide a comprehensive, end-to-end suite of IT services tailored to meet the complex needs of modern global enterprises, leveraging our Global Delivery Model (GDM) to ensure cost-effectiveness and high quality.

## 1. Cloud Transformation & Migration
We enable organizations to seamlessly transition their legacy on-premise infrastructure to robust, scalable cloud environments (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud).
- **Cloud Readiness Assessment**: Thorough evaluation of existing IT landscapes to develop a strategic migration roadmap.
- **Lift and Shift Migration**: Executing seamless workload transitions with stringent SLAs and minimal business disruption.
- **Cloud-Native Modernization**: Refactoring monolithic applications into scalable microservices and serverless architectures.

## 2. Enterprise Application Services
Our vast pool of engineering talent builds, maintains, and modernizes secure, resilient enterprise applications.
- **Custom Application Development**: Delivering bespoke, responsive digital solutions leveraging our Centers of Excellence (CoEs).
- **Enterprise Integration & API Development**: Connecting disparate enterprise systems (ERP, CRM) to create unified workflows.
- **Legacy System Modernization**: Upgrading mission-critical legacy software to modern technology stacks to ensure future-readiness.

## 3. Data Analytics & Artificial Intelligence
Unlock actionable insights from your enterprise data with our advanced analytics and AI capabilities.
- **Enterprise Data Management**: Centralizing organizational data into robust Data Warehousing and Data Lake solutions.
- **Generative AI & Machine Learning**: Implementing Custom LLMs, RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipelines, and predictive models.
- **Business Intelligence**: Deploying intuitive dashboards and reporting tools for data-driven decision-making.

## 4. Cybersecurity, Risk & Compliance
Safeguarding your digital assets while ensuring adherence to global regulatory frameworks.
- **Vulnerability Assessments & Penetration Testing (VAPT)**: Proactive identification and mitigation of security vulnerabilities.
- **Compliance & Audit Readiness**: Ensuring rigorous adherence to GDPR, HIPAA, SOC 2, and ISO 27001 standards.
- **Managed Security Operations Center (SOC)**: Providing 24/7/365 continuous monitoring, threat detection, and incident response.
