# New Associate Onboarding & Integration Guide

## Day 1: System Access & Documentation

- **Portal Login**: All new hires must log in to the **MyRSystems employee portal** on Day 1 to complete mandatory employment documentation.
- **HR Details**: Navigate to the **MyHR section** to review leave balances, tax declaration forms, and set up your direct deposit for payroll.
- **ID Card**: Collect your official R Systems ID badge from the front desk upon arrival. Your badge is required for building access at all ODCs.

## Mandatory Training & Compliance

- **InfoSec Training**: All associates must complete the **Annual Information Security (InfoSec) and Data Privacy training** modules within the first 14 days of joining.
- **Phishing Awareness**: All employees must participate in periodic phishing simulation exercises. Failure to pass these simulations will result in mandatory remedial training sessions.
- **Code of Conduct**: Review the "Dos and Don'ts" policy, specifically regarding client data confidentiality and workstation security.

## Office Attendance & Logistics

- **Mandatory In-Office Days**: To foster collaboration, in-office presence is mandatory every **Tuesday and Thursday** at all India locations (Bangalore, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai).
- **Remote Work Policy**: Outside of mandatory office days, Work From Home (WFH) is permitted up to 2 days per week, subject to prior approval from your assigned Delivery Manager.
- **Connectivity**: When working remotely, you are strictly required to use the R Systems Corporate VPN to access client environments. Accessing client data via unsecured public Wi-Fi is explicitly forbidden.

## Onboarding Support

- **Buddy Program**: Every new joiner is assigned a "Buddy" from their respective team to assist with technical setup and cultural integration during the first 30 days.
- **Escalation Matrix**: Familiarize yourself with the escalation matrix for project-related issues, which is available in your team's project repository.
- **IT Support**: For hardware issues (e.g., MacBook Pro setup or peripheral requests), please raise a P1 or P2 ticket via the **RSystems Global Service Desk portal**.
