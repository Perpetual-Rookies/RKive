# Associate Handbook - IT Policies & Procedures

## Acceptable Use of IT Resources
Company-provided IT assets, software, and network resources are strictly designated for official business purposes. Personal use is heavily restricted and must not compromise enterprise security or productivity.
- Associates are strictly prohibited from installing unapproved or pirated software on company-provisioned devices.
- Access to client environments and confidential data must only be initiated over a secure, encrypted connection (e.g., RSystems Corporate VPN) using authorized credentials.

## Data Security & Privacy
As a trusted IT services partner to global enterprises, safeguarding our clients' intellectual property and data is our paramount responsibility.
- **Password Policy**: Passwords must be a minimum of 14 characters, containing a complex mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters. Passwords must be rotated every 60 days.
- **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)**: Mandatory enforcement for all corporate accounts, email access, client VDIs, and internal platforms.
- **Security Awareness**: All associates are mandated to complete the Annual Information Security (InfoSec) and Data Privacy training modules. Phishing simulation failures will result in mandatory remedial training.

## Remote Work & ODC Policy
RSystems Ltd. operates under strict compliance guidelines, especially when associates access client data from Offshore Development Centers (ODCs) or approved Work From Home (WFH) locations.
- Work environments must ensure absolute privacy. Shoulder surfing or unauthorized viewing of client data must be prevented at all times.
- Connecting to unsecured public Wi-Fi networks is explicitly forbidden.
- Workstations must be locked (Windows Key + L) immediately when left unattended, regardless of location.

## Incident Management & Reporting
Any suspected security breach, loss of IT assets, or unauthorized data access must be reported to the Global IT Security team within 30 minutes of discovery.
- Contact the 24/7 Global SOC hotline at +91-800-555-0199 for critical information security incidents.
- Raise an immediate P1 ticket via the RSystems Global Service Desk portal for all other compliance and security concerns.
