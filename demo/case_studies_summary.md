# Case Studies & Success Stories

## Case Study 1: Legacy Core Banking Transformation for a Global Financial Institution

**Client Industry**: Banking & Financial Services (BFSI)
**Challenge**: A leading North American bank was encumbered by aging on-premise mainframe systems, resulting in exorbitant maintenance costs, rigid architectures, and delayed time-to-market for new digital banking features.
**Solution**: Leveraging our Global Delivery Model, RSystems Ltd. orchestrated a multi-year, phased migration to Microsoft Azure. Our Cloud Center of Excellence (CoE) containerized legacy monolithic applications using Docker and Kubernetes, and implemented an automated CI/CD pipeline.
**Results**:

- Achieved a 40% reduction in Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) for IT infrastructure.
- Release cycles were accelerated from quarterly deployments to bi-weekly sprints.
- Ensured 99.999% high availability during peak transaction volumes.

## Case Study 2: Intelligent Automation in Customer Service for a Retail Major

**Client Industry**: Retail & Consumer Goods
**Challenge**: A Fortune 500 retail corporation faced immense pressure during peak holiday seasons, with their customer support teams overwhelmed by escalating ticket volumes, leading to degraded SLAs and customer dissatisfaction.
**Solution**: RSystems Ltd. designed and deployed a sophisticated Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) virtual assistant. The solution was rigorously trained on the client's proprietary product catalogs, historical resolution data, and standard operating procedures (SOPs).
**Results**:

- The AI-driven virtual assistant autonomously resolved 65% of Tier-1 support inquiries.
- Mean Time to Resolution (MTTR) plummeted from 12 hours to under 2 minutes.
- Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) index saw a remarkable improvement of 22%.

## Case Study 3: Enterprise Security Posture Enhancement for a Healthcare Provider

**Client Industry**: Healthcare & Life Sciences
**Challenge**: Following a minor localized data exposure, a large healthcare network urgently required a comprehensive overhaul of their security infrastructure to guarantee stringent HIPAA compliance and safeguard Electronic Health Records (EHR).
**Solution**: RSystems Ltd.'s cybersecurity practice architected and deployed a Zero-Trust network model. We enforced mandatory MFA across the enterprise and onboarded the client to our 24/7 Managed Security Operations Center (SOC) based out of our Bangalore facility.
**Results**:

- Successfully cleared all subsequent independent HIPAA and HITRUST audits with zero non-conformities.
- Proactively identified and neutralized 3 sophisticated ransomware campaigns in the first 12 months.
- Delivered customized InfoSec training to over 2,000 healthcare professionals and administrative staff.
